Machine Learning

An undergraduate course at the National University of Kyiv-Mohyla Academy

Dmytro Kuzmenko, Assistant Professor, Department of Multimedia Systems · kuzmenko@ukma.edu.ua

120 hours in total, worth 4 credits in the European Credit Transfer System. Forty of those hours are spent in class, as ten lectures and ten practical sessions, and roughly eighty are spent working independently. The course concludes with a pass or fail credit.

This course teaches you to formulate, train, evaluate, diagnose and explain an ordinary machine-learning system, working from regression and classification through to decision trees, support vector machines, clustering and small neural networks. The emphasis throughout falls on method: what an experiment actually measures, and why its result can be trusted. It does not fall on the syntax of any particular library.

How the grade is made up

The final grade has four components, described in full on the assessment page.

Homework 30% Midterm 15% Mini-project 25% Final examination 30%

The homework component is six assignments worth five points each. The course concludes with a pass or fail credit, and at least 30 points on the university scale are required before you may sit the final assessment.

What the course is for

The single ability this course sets out to build is the ability to formulate, train, evaluate, diagnose and explain an ordinary machine-learning system. Artificial-intelligence assistants make the implementation faster — writing code, finding bugs, phrasing an explanation — but they do not take the place of understanding the material yourself. You are expected to justify every methodological decision in your work, and that expectation is enforced through short oral checks and through the examinations.

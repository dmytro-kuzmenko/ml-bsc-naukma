Practical sessions

Ten sessions that build methodological judgment and prepare you for the examinations

The practical sessions develop the way you think about method: reading a plot, diagnosing a model, choosing a metric and a validation strategy, and noticing data leakage. They are not graded. What they do is prepare you for the homework assignments, for the mini-project and for the examinations.

Some sessions are discussions rather than coding exercises. The notebooks are written for self-study: open them on your own machine, or in Google Colab using the buttons that appear beneath every link.

The overview of the practical sessions describes how each notebook is organized and how to open it.

The ten notebooks

Practical 1 · Week 1

Designing an experiment, and the simplest forms of leakage

The discipline of separating training data from test data, what a baseline is for, and how to recognize data leakage in its most obvious forms.

Practical 4 · Week 4

Practical 5 · Week 5

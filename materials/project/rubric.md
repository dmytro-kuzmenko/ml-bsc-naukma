# Mini-project: grading rubric (25 points)

There are eight criteria and each one is scored from 0 to 5, giving a raw total of 40. The raw total is converted to the 25 points the mini-project contributes to the course by multiplying it by five eighths, that is, by 0.625.

The presentation is not graded on its own. A polished dashboard built on weak method cannot earn a high score.

| Criterion | 5 points, excellent | 3 points, satisfactory | 0 to 1 points, unsatisfactory |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Formulating the problem** | The question is specific and clear, it is obvious why it matters, and it is obvious what would count as an answer | A question is present, but it is vague or trivial | There is no question, or the question cannot be understood |
| **Method** | The models and the preprocessing are chosen for stated reasons, and everything is compared against a baseline | The choices are made, but no reason is given for them | The models appear to have been picked at random, and there is no baseline |
| **Validity of the experiment** | The split or the cross-validation scheme matches the structure of the data, nothing leaks, and the pipeline is used correctly | The split is broadly correct, with minor flaws | Leakage or an invalid split has distorted the conclusions |
| **Evaluation** | The metrics suit the task, and more than one number is shown, in tables or curves | The metrics are appropriate, but the analysis stops at the surface | The wrong metric is used, or accuracy is reported without any context |
| **Analysis and interpretation** | The results are interpreted, the conclusions follow from the evidence, and the limitations are named | The interpretation is partial | The conclusions are not supported by the results |
| **Analysis of errors** | It is clear where the model fails and why, with examples and identified patterns | The errors are noted but not examined | There is no analysis of errors |
| **Reproducibility** | Seeds are fixed, the notebook runs from top to bottom, and the environment is documented | The work is partly reproducible | The work cannot be reproduced |
| **Quality of the conclusions** | The conclusions are careful, they match the evidence, and nothing is overclaimed | Mostly sound, with some overclaiming | The conclusions bear no relation to the results |

## The default scale

The bands below may be adjusted at the defense.

| Raw total, out of 40 | Points awarded, out of 25 | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 36 to 40 | 22.5 to 25 | Excellent |
| 29 to 35 | 18 to 22 | Good |
| 23 to 28 | 14.5 to 17.5 | Satisfactory |
| Below 23 | Below 14.5 | Revision required |

## What is deliberately not graded

- The number of plots, and how attractive the presentation looks.
- The use of the newest or the most complicated model available.
- Reaching a record score.

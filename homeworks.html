Homework assignments

Six assignments worth five points each, together 30 percent of the final grade

Each assignment is a small but complete study, and each one follows the same five steps: you run an experiment, you make one meaningful methodological decision, you produce evidence in the form of a plot, a table or a metric, you give the interpretation that the evidence supports, and you state a counterfactual by predicting what would change if a parameter or an assumption were different.

Every submission must include a disclosure of the artificial-intelligence tools you used. Any submission may be selected for a short oral check of two to five minutes; the course policies describe how that works.

Late submissions. You have five late days to spread across all six assignments as you wish, and one late day covers up to 24 hours. Once those days are used up, the maximum score falls by 25 percent for every further 24 hours. The policies page gives the full rule.

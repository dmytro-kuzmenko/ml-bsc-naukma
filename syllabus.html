Machine Learning · spring semester · 4 credits in the European Credit Transfer System

Dmytro Kuzmenko, Assistant Professor, Department of Multimedia Systems · kuzmenko@ukma.edu.ua

How much work the course involves

MeasureAmount
Total workload120 hours, worth 4 credits in the European Credit Transfer System
Hours spent in class40 hours: ten lectures and ten practical sessions, each of two academic hours
Hours of independent workApproximately 80
Final form of assessmentA credit, awarded as a pass or a fail

How the grade is calculated

ComponentWhat it consists ofPointsShare of the grade
Homework assignmentsSix assignments, five points each3030%
Midterm examinationAn individual test on DistEdu, about 30 minutes long1515%
Mini-projectOne complete investigation, alone or in a pair2525%
Final examinationA cumulative test on DistEdu, about 45 minutes long3030%
Total100100%

At least 30 points on the university scale are required before you may sit the final assessment. The assessment page explains each component in detail, and the course policies set out the rules that govern them.

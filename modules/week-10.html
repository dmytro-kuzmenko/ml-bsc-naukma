Week 10 of 10

Machine Learning in Practice

The complete workflow from question to conclusion, the mini-project checklist, reproducibility, and the defense.

Practical session 10 No homework this week Final examination, at the end of the course

What this module covers

  • The workflow from beginning to end: the question, the baseline, the pipeline, the evaluation, the diagnosis
  • The eleven-step checklist that structures the mini-project
  • Reproducibility: fixed seeds, an unambiguous order of execution, and recorded library versions
  • Communicating a result, together with its limitations and its conclusions
  • The mini-project defense: five to seven minutes for a pair, with a question put to each student individually

What you will be able to do afterwards

By the end of this module you should be able to:

  • carry a small study through from the original question to the final conclusions
  • apply the checklist of method to your own mini-project
  • make your work reproducible and evaluate it honestly
  • defend a result: justify each decision, and answer a question about what would change under other circumstances

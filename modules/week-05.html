Week 5 of 10

Margins, Similarity and Kernels

Margins, support vector machines, feature scaling, kernels, and the trade-off between C and gamma.

Practical session 5 No homework this week Midterm examination, after Module 5

What this module covers

  • Linear separators, and what the margin between the classes means
  • The support vector machine, and maximizing that margin as a principle
  • Why feature scaling matters so much more here than it does for a tree
  • Kernels, both linear and the radial basis function, and when a kernel actually helps
  • The hyperparameters C and gamma, seen through what each of them would change

What you will be able to do afterwards

By the end of this module you should be able to:

  • explain what a margin is, and why a support vector machine maximizes it
  • demonstrate why feature scaling is decisive for this family of models
  • choose between a linear and a radial basis function kernel from the shape of the data
  • predict what a change to C or to gamma would do to the boundary

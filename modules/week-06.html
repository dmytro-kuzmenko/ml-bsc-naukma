Week 6 of 10

Evaluation Done Properly

Cross-validation, stratification, leakage between groups, pipelines, and the two kinds of data leakage.

Practical session 6 Homework 4 Final examination, at the end of the course

What this module covers

  • Why a single split into training and test data is unreliable, and how much the estimate varies
  • Cross-validation: splitting into k folds, and stratifying those folds when a class is rare
  • Leakage between groups, and the grouped split that prevents it
  • Pipelines, and why scaling belongs inside the cross-validation loop rather than before it
  • Leakage through preprocessing, and leakage through the target
  • Tuning hyperparameters without ever looking at the test set

What you will be able to do afterwards

By the end of this module you should be able to:

  • explain why a single split gives an unreliable estimate of quality
  • choose the validation strategy that suits the structure of the data: plain, stratified or grouped
  • build a pipeline in which the scaler never sees the test data
  • find leakage through preprocessing and through the target in somebody else's code

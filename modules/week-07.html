Week 7 of 10

Finding Structure Without Labels

k-means, choosing the number of clusters, feature scaling, principal component analysis, and the limits of clustering by distance.

Practical session 7 Homework 5 Final examination, at the end of the course

What this module covers

  • Learning without labels, and why it is worth looking for structure when no answers are given
  • k-means: the algorithm itself, how it is initialized, and why it converges
  • Choosing the number of clusters, using inertia and the silhouette score
  • The effect of feature scaling on a method that works entirely by distance
  • Principal component analysis: the components themselves, the scree plot, and a projection into two dimensions
  • The limits of clustering by distance, seen most clearly on data arranged in rings

What you will be able to do afterwards

By the end of this module you should be able to:

  • explain what k-means does, and how to choose the number of clusters honestly
  • show how feature scaling changes the result of clustering
  • use principal component analysis both to visualize data and to reduce its dimension
  • explain the situations in which clustering by distance fails as a matter of principle

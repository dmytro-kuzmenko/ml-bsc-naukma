Week 4 of 10

Trees and Ensembles

Decision trees, random forests, gradient boosting, and how to read feature importances without being misled.

Practical session 4 Homework 3 Midterm examination, after Module 5

What this module covers

  • Decision trees: the rules they learn, the effect of depth, and how they overfit
  • The complexity curve for a tree, drawn against its maximum depth
  • Random forests, and where their stability actually comes from
  • Gradient boosting, and how it differs from averaging
  • Feature importance: what it genuinely shows, and the circumstances in which it lies

What you will be able to do afterwards

By the end of this module you should be able to:

  • explain why a deep tree overfits
  • justify why a random forest generalizes better than any single tree in it
  • compare a tree, a forest and a boosted model on honestly measured training and validation scores
  • interpret a table of feature importances with appropriate caution
